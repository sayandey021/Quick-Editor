Quick Editor Privacy Policy
Effective Date: October 2, 2026
Last Updated: October 2, 2026

Quick Editor is a lightweight desktop media editor designed for trimming, cropping, color grading, and real-time audio editing of video and audio files. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what data Quick Editor collects, how it is used, and your rights as a user.

Information We Collect:
No Personal Data by Default: Quick Editor does not require you to create an account or provide personal information to use any of its features.

Media & Editing Data (Local Only): Your opened media files, trim selections, audio adjustments, custom settings, and theme preferences are processed and stored strictly locally on your device. Quick Editor does not transmit your files, edits, or media data to us or to any remote servers.

Error Reports (Optional): If you choose to submit issues or contact support, you may voluntarily share logs or system details to help troubleshoot problems.

How We Use Information:
To improve app performance and fix bugs (via optional error reports).

To provide support when you contact us.

Data Sharing:
Quick Editor does not sell, rent, or share your personal data or media files with third parties.

Any data you voluntarily share in public forums or issue trackers is visible to the community.

Security:
Quick Editor runs 100% locally on your device. All media rendering and processing happens entirely offline.

We recommend downloading releases only from the official Microsoft Store or our official repository to ensure authenticity and avoid compromised software.

Your Rights:
You have complete control over all data and files handled by Quick Editor on your device.

Quick Editor never alters your original files unless you choose to overwrite them. You may delete exported media or uninstall Quick Editor at any time to completely remove the application and its local data.
